# Vendor Risk Assessment Report

## Overview
This project simulates a third-party risk management process by assessing mock vendors based on data sensitivity, business criticality, and security control maturity.

## Vendors Assessed
- CloudDocs Pro
- PayFlow HR
- AlertStack

## Assessment Criteria
- Data sensitivity
- Business criticality
- Access level
- Security controls
- Compliance posture

## Key Findings

### CloudDocs Pro
- High inherent risk due to storage of sensitive company files
- Moderate control maturity
- Additional assurance documentation recommended

### PayFlow HR
- High inherent risk due to employee PII processing
- Higher residual risk because of limited available assurance evidence
- Requires stronger due diligence and contractual security terms

### AlertStack
- Medium inherent risk
- Lower residual risk due to better security posture and reduced data sensitivity
- Acceptable with annual reassessment

## Recommendations
- Obtain assurance documentation such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001 where available
- Include security and privacy clauses in contracts
- Reassess high-risk vendors annually
- Track open findings through remediation plans

## Outcome
This exercise demonstrates the ability to perform vendor due diligence, apply risk tiering, assess residual risk, and communicate findings in a structured GRC workflow.
